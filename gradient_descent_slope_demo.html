What to watch

  1. The dot's height is the cost. It tells you how bad the current w is.
  2. The orange tangent and dotted triangle give the slope: move 1 step right (run), see how much the line goes up or down (rise).
  3. The green arrow is the step. It is α × slope. Steep curve → tall triangle → long arrow.
  4. Press "Take one step" several times. α never changes, but the steps shrink because the curve gets flatter near the bottom.
  5. Start on the left side: the rise is negative, so the arrow points right. Either way it moves toward the minimum.
  6. Try α near 0.9: the steps overshoot and bounce across the bowl. Too big a learning rate causes this.
